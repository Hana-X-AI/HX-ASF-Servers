What changed

Replaced Meta-X (Muse Glimmer 30B) with OAI-X (gpt-oss-20b) as the factory's tooling/structured-contracts LLM on hxs-3. The Ollama operating-profile alias hx-muse-glimmer-64k was superseded by hx-oai-x-64k.

Why

Meta-X was too slow for LightRAG entity extraction. The root cause was an OmniRoute 504 timeout (RATE_LIMIT_EXECUTION_TIMEOUT) during LightRAG's entity-extraction prompts. OAI-X (gpt-oss-20b) provides tool-calling and structured JSON output at acceptable latency for the factory's tooling workloads.
